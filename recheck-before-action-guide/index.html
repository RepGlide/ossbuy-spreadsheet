Recheck Before Action Guide

What to re-view before acting, and when a conflict, gap, or redirect says pause.

Act last, recheck first

The page is the last thing you look at before acting. Re-read it fresh, then decide.

What to re-view

  • Page identity — is this the page you meant to reach?
  • Visible options — are the color, size, and version still selectable?
  • Price context — is the currency and time still clear?
  • Stock and availability — is the item still shown as available?

When to pause

Conflict

The page and the sheet disagree. Pause and record both.

Missing detail

An option or a price is gone. Pause and re-read before continuing.

Redirect

The link lands somewhere unexpected. Pause and note the real destination.

?Open item: is there any conflict, gap, or redirect still unresolved before you act?

A worked recheck

  1. Before acting: the page is re-read.
  2. Found: the size is no longer selectable.
  3. Action: pause and re-read before continuing.
