A Handoff Card

One Card from Sheet to Page

A single note carries the lead through the relay. It is a reference card, not a form or a board.

From

The sheet row and its visible label.

To

The destination the link actually opens.

Carry

Category and the current label — nothing changeable.

Recheck

Options, price, stock, and page identity.

Stop if

The link is missing, redirected, or in conflict.

Broken Relay Signals

When the Relay Breaks

A relay that breaks mid-way is a signal to pause, not to guess.

  • 1

    Missing link. The lead has no working destination. Pause and locate one.

  • 2

    Redirect. The link lands somewhere unexpected. Pause and note the real destination.

  • 3

    Context change. The page no longer matches the category. Pause and re-read.

  • 4

    Conflict. The page and the sheet disagree. Pause and record both.

The Finish Line Is a Fresh Check

Arriving Is Not the Same as Verifying

Reaching the destination page is the start of the final check, not the end of it.

  • 1

    Fresh look. Re-read the page as it is now, not as the sheet described it.

  • 2

    Recheck details. Options, price, stock, and identity are checked again here.

  • 3

    Then act. Only after a fresh check do you continue to the external page.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an OSSBuy spreadsheet?

An OSSBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a reference to follow, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official OSSBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by OSSBuy or Google.

Does the research relay track or verify anything?

No. The research relay is a handoff routine. It is not a product database, store, QC certification, shopping agent, order tracker, or fee calculator. It does not provide live tracking or verification.

What should I carry forward in the handoff?

Carry only the category and the current visible label. Everything changeable — options, price, stock, and page identity — is rechecked at the destination, not carried.

What should I recheck on the destination page?

Recheck the page identity, the visible options, any context changes, and where the link actually landed before taking any external action.

Where do I place an order?

After you have rechecked the destination page, you continue on an external page to submit the item. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
