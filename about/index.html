About This Resource

A research relay and editorial track built around a shared OSSBuy spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use an OSSBuy spreadsheet. It offers a research relay — sheet cue, category context, destination page, and current confirmation — so each lead passes forward only the context that was actually seen.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official OSSBuy website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by OSSBuy or Google. It is not a product database, store, QC certification, shopping agent, order tracker, or fee calculator.

The research relay

Everything here follows a simple rule: carry only the category and the current label; recheck options, price, stock, and page identity at the destination; leave behind old conclusions and numbers without a source.

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product categories from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any listing clue.
  4. Follow each link through a clean handoff to a fresh check.

Research example — not live tracking, verification, or saved activity.
