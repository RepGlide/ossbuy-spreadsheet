Research Handoff Guide

The four legs of the relay: sheet cue, category context, destination page, and current confirmation.

Pass only what was seen

Each leg of the relay passes forward only what it actually saw. Changeable details are never carried — they are rechecked at the next leg.

1. Sheet cue

Record what the row currently shows. The cue is a starting point, not a conclusion.

2. Category context

Keep which category the cue belongs to. The category is the stable context you carry forward.

3. Destination page

Look at where the link actually opens now. This is the current fact, not the remembered one.

4. Current confirmation

Recheck the specific options and the changeable detail on the new page. This is where the lead settles.
